Privacy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This page explains the account and service data AgentSecret handles when you use sign-in, connect a secret manager, or authorize an API request.

Information we handle

  • Sign-in information. GitHub sign-in requests read:user and user:email. Google sign-in requests openid, email, and profile. AgentSecret uses the provider's stable account identifier to authenticate you. Your name, avatar, and email may be stored as account profile details; email is optional and is not used by itself to link accounts. Provider access tokens are used during sign-in and are not retained as account credentials.
  • Account and configuration data. This includes your chosen display name, connected-provider configuration, secret references, aliases, access policies, and account settings. A connected provider configuration is encrypted at rest.
  • Usage and security records. To enforce your policies and investigate service failures, AgentSecret records authorized-operation counts and limited request metadata such as time, alias, HTTP method, destination host, and result status. Recent request audit entries are retained for up to seven days.

Secret values and API requests

When an enabled provider connection is used, AgentSecret retrieves the selected value to carry out an operation allowed by your account policy. The value is not stored in request audit records or included in standard agent-facing results. An authorized request is sent to the destination you configured, so that destination receives the request data required to perform the operation.

Cloudflare runs the AgentSecret Worker and account authority. AgentSecret's production account and identity registry is hosted by Turso Cloud in Singapore. GitHub or Google handles sign-in; connected secret managers and API destinations process information when you authorize their use. Their own privacy policies also apply.

Cookies

AgentSecret uses secure, HTTP-only cookies for account sessions and short-lived sign-in transactions. These cookies support login, account linking, and logout; they are not advertising cookies. A customer session expires within eight hours.

Retention and account deletion

Account information and connected configuration remain while the account is active. Expired sessions and short-lived transaction data are discarded. Recent request audit entries are retained for up to seven days.

When an account is deleted, its profile, connected configuration, policies, and ordinary account records are removed. AgentSecret keeps minimal account and provider-identity tombstones to prevent a deleted identity or stale request from recreating access. These tombstones include the provider identifier and deletion time; the current service does not automatically expire them.

For help with your account or a privacy request, contact info@astronlab.com.
